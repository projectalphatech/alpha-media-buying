# Organic → paid bridge

Regular organic content (posts, reels) can create **warm audiences**. This file decides when and how paid spend touches them. Researched 2026-10-23.

## Funnel and budget split

```
Cold:    lead campaign (location + age controls)                        ~80% of spend
Warm:    boost best organic reels → video viewers / engagers             ~10-20%
Retarget: lead ads to warm audiences, excluding customers/leads          from warm budget once pools are big enough
```
Split is `[HYPOTHESIS]`. Warm spend starts small (for example 10-15% of the cold daily budget) and only after the gates below.

## Gate 1 - when to boost a piece

Boost only a piece that, after 48 h organic:
- is in the **top 2 of the week** on hold rate (avg watch / % watched), and
- has a hook rate at or above the 4-week median, and
- passes your content QA (offer matches the live ad, brand rules).

One boosted piece at a time per pillar. Never boost a piece because it got likes.

## How to boost (Ads Manager, not the "Boost" button) `[PRACTICE]`

- Use an **existing post** as the ad (keeps the organic likes/comments together).
- Objective: **Engagement → video views, maximise ThruPlay** for audience building; or **Awareness → reach** with a frequency cap for pure local recall. Verify the exact performance-goal names in Ads Manager: third-party guides say ThruPlay sits under Engagement and Awareness uses a 2-second view goal `[C, unverified with Meta]`.
- Same location + minimum-age controls as the cold campaign (same areas and age controls).
- Small daily budget, 5-7 days, then stop. Judge on cost per ThruPlay and on the size of the audience built, not on leads.
- The Ads Manager route keeps the controls; the in-app Boost button gives fewer settings `[PRACTICE]`.

## Warm audiences to create (once, then they fill themselves)

| Audience | Source | Retention | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video 50%+ viewers | FB Page + IG videos (organic and boosted) | 365 d max `[B: several secondary sources, Meta page not read]` | main warm pool |
| Video 95% viewers | same | 365 d | hottest, small |
| IG professional account engagers | your IG account | 365 d | needs IG business/creator account `[B]` |
| FB Page engagers | your Page | 365 d | |
| Messaged the Page/WhatsApp | Page engagement → messaged | 365 d | for exclusion as much as targeting |
| Customers + active leads | customer list from the CRM (hashed phones/emails) | n/a | **always excluded** from cold and warm prospecting |

Retention windows are chosen in Ads Manager when the audience is created; check the dropdown maximum there and record it in `platform-state.md`. Purchase website audiences went to 730 days (2026-05-18) - usually not relevant to lead businesses `[B]`.

## Gate 2 - when to retarget

- Warm pool (video 50% + engagers, minus customers/leads) **≥ ~10,000 people** `[PRACTICE]`. Below that, a daily budget just buys frequency (see `strategy.md`).
- Then: one click-to-WhatsApp ad set targeting the warm pool, separate campaign or ad set from cold so results stay readable. Creative: proof or price pieces, the objection answers.
- Frequency watch: pause or refresh when 7-day frequency > ~4 with falling CTR `[PRACTICE]`.

## How to judge the bridge

- Leads from warm vs cold: cost per **qualified** lead (Q2) and cost per paid client, from the CRM, not Ads Manager.
- Attribution: unique pre-filled WhatsApp text per campaign (warm vs cold) so the CRM can tag the source.
- Window: 4 weeks minimum before deciding warm spend is or isn't worth it; organic effects are slow.

## Not doing (yet)

- Lookalikes of customers when you have only a handful: far below a useful seed (Meta's minimum is 100 people) `[OFFICIAL-ish, verify]`.
- Engagement-objective campaigns optimising for likes/comments: they buy cheap engagers, the same failure as cheap taps.
